About SpaceBattles

SpaceBattles is a group of authors, readers, and enthusiasts who have been passionate about storytelling since 1996. Whether you are here to read engrossing tales, participate in conversations, or offer your own artistic creations, we encourage you to investigate the various worlds created by our members. Additionally, we participate in a fair bit of talk regarding hypothetical situations and the actual world. The members and staff welcome individuals from all walks of life and backgrounds, but disruptions and bad faith are not tolerated. For many, what we've constructed is an "internet home" is located here. Since its founding in 1996, our community has expanded dramatically since 2009 and changed hands three times.
